Most votes were not party-line

Each column counts roll calls by the gap between the share of Republicans and the share of Democrats who voted yes. On 1,634 of the 3,546 (46%), the two parties were within 10 points of each other, and 932 were unanimous. On 136 (4%), they were 80 points or more apart.

Three 2026 races, and what the record shows

In each race, one candidate sat in the Texas House in 2025 and has votes on these bills, and the other did not. Each page says exactly what the record shows for both, and where the two kinds of record stop being comparable.

  • Governor: Gina Hinojosa and Greg Abbott
    Why Greg Abbott has no votes here: Has never served in a legislature, so he has no roll-call record at all.
    What the record shows for both
  • Lieutenant Governor: Vikki Goodwin and Dan Patrick
    Why Dan Patrick has no votes here: Presides over the Senate rather than voting in the House, so he casts no vote on these bills.
    What the record shows for both
  • U.S. Senate: James Talarico and Ken Paxton
    Why Ken Paxton has no votes here: His last legislative vote was in 2015, before any of the three took office — there is no session they share.
