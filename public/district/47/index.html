Vikki Goodwin, Texas House District 47

Vikki Goodwin represented Texas House District 47 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Vikki Goodwin cast 64, and on the 49 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.
