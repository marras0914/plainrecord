Stan Gerdes, Texas House District 17

Stan Gerdes represented Texas House District 17 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Stan Gerdes cast 64, and on the 49 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 4 times.

Where District 17 is

District 17 spans 5 counties, and 202,973 people lived in it at the 2020 Census: Bastrop County (48%), Caldwell County (23%), Milam County (12%), Burleson County (9%) and Lee County (9%).

Most of District 17 (55%) lives outside any city or named community, in unincorporated parts of those counties.
