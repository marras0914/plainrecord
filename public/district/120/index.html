Barbara Gervin-Hawkins, Texas House District 120

Barbara Gervin-Hawkins represented Texas House District 120 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Barbara Gervin-Hawkins cast 42, and on the 33 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.
