Venton Jones, Texas House District 100

Venton Jones represented Texas House District 100 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Venton Jones cast 56, and on the 47 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 1 time.
