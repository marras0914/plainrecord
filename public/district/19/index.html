Ellen Troxclair, Texas House District 19

Ellen Troxclair represented Texas House District 19 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Ellen Troxclair cast 64, and on the 50 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.
