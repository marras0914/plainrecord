Tom Oliverson, Texas House District 130

Tom Oliverson represented Texas House District 130 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Tom Oliverson cast 65, and on the 49 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.

Where District 130 is

District 130 is in Harris County, and 192,603 people lived in it at the 2020 Census.

Most of District 130 (92%) lives outside any city or named community, in unincorporated Harris County.
