Gina Hinojosa, Texas House District 49

Gina Hinojosa represented Texas House District 49 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Gina Hinojosa cast 62, and on the 48 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.
