Trent Ashby, Texas House District 9

Trent Ashby represented Texas House District 9 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Trent Ashby cast 66, and on the 51 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 8 times.

Where District 9 is

District 9 spans 6 counties, and 199,902 people lived in it at the 2020 Census: Angelina County (43%), Polk County (25%), Houston County (11%), Tyler County (10%), Trinity County (7%) and San Augustine County (4%).

Where they broke with their own party

  • HB 5616 — Creates a state program and fund to promote the presidential libraries in Texas as historical and tourist attractions. history
  • HB 3335 — Lets a county donate surplus property to a civic or charitable group without first showing that an auction would fail, but requires it instead to find that the county can keep enough control to ensure the donation serves its public purpose. history
  • SB 1498 — Lets the state seize cryptocurrency through civil forfeiture, by adding digital currency to the property that can be taken as suspected proceeds of a crime. history
  • SB 2185 — Requires school districts excused from running a bilingual programme to report which alternative language methods they use, so those methods count towards bilingual education funding. history
  • HB 5111 — Sets who may serve as a campaign treasurer: at least 18, a Texas resident, not already treasurer for someone else, and not convicted of certain election offences. history
  • HB 3892 — Stops counties from limiting how many homes can be built per acre outside city limits, including through minimum lot sizes, lot widths or setbacks. history
  • HB 3178 — Lets the county where the Guadalupe River rises charge its own hotel tax, except at hotels a city already taxes. history
  • HB 2298 — Creates a state grant programme to help Texas hospitals and clinics use artificial intelligence to scan medical images for cancer. history
