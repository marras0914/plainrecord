Cody Harris, Texas House District 8

Cody Harris represented Texas House District 8 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Cody Harris cast 40, and on the 33 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 11 times.

Where District 8 is

District 8 spans 4 counties, and 192,599 people lived in it at the 2020 Census: Anderson County (30%), Navarro County (27%), Cherokee County (26%) and Henderson County (16%).

Most of District 8 (55%) lives outside any city or named community, in unincorporated parts of those counties.

Where they broke with their own party

  • HB 2284 — Requires music therapists to hold a state licence before they can practise, and allows the state to charge fees for it. history
  • HB 2446 — For medical bills of $50,000 or less, removes the need for a sworn affidavit to prove the charges were reasonable and the care was necessary. history
  • HB 2341 — Makes time credit automatic, rather than up to the judge, for state jail inmates who take part in an education, treatment or work programme. history
  • HB 5111 — Sets who may serve as a campaign treasurer: at least 18, a Texas resident, not already treasurer for someone else, and not convicted of certain election offences. history
  • HB 1203 — Creates an office inside the Department of Agriculture responsible for keeping the state's food supply secure and free of disruption. history
  • HB 2234 — Raises the age at which someone is tried as an adult from 17 to 18, so 17-year-olds go through the juvenile system instead. It applies only to conduct on or after 1 September 2027. history
  • HB 5525 — Requires the parole board to write separate guidelines for people convicted of crimes they committed before turning 18, weighing rehabilitation and education. history
  • SB 1563 — Requires the state to create training for county jailers on dealing with veterans in custody. history
  • HB 2186 — Requires elevator mechanics, apprentices and contractors to register with the state before working on elevators. history
  • HB 2298 — Creates a state grant programme to help Texas hospitals and clinics use artificial intelligence to scan medical images for cancer. history
  • HB 3947 — Requires regional transport planners to take account of the travel needs of people who use state health, welfare and workforce services. history
