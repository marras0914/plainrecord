Helen Kerwin, Texas House District 58

Helen Kerwin represented Texas House District 58 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Helen Kerwin cast 50, and on the 38 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.
