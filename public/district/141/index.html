Senfronia Thompson, Texas House District 141

Senfronia Thompson represented Texas House District 141 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Senfronia Thompson cast 65, and on the 49 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.

Where District 141 is

District 141 is in Harris County, and 201,495 people lived in it at the 2020 Census.

  • Houston 48% of the district · 4% of Houston’s residents
  • Humble 7% of the district · 79% of Humble’s residents
