Ana Hernandez, Texas House District 143

Ana Hernandez represented Texas House District 143 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Ana Hernandez cast 57, and on the 44 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 1 time.
