Mitch Little, Texas House District 65

Mitch Little represented Texas House District 65 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Mitch Little cast 61, and on the 47 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 8 times.

Where they broke with their own party

  • HB 2284 — Requires music therapists to hold a state licence before they can practise, and allows the state to charge fees for it. history
  • HB 2341 — Makes time credit automatic, rather than up to the judge, for state jail inmates who take part in an education, treatment or work programme. history
  • SB 1498 — Lets the state seize cryptocurrency through civil forfeiture, by adding digital currency to the property that can be taken as suspected proceeds of a crime. history
  • SB 2185 — Requires school districts excused from running a bilingual programme to report which alternative language methods they use, so those methods count towards bilingual education funding. history
  • HB 5525 — Requires the parole board to write separate guidelines for people convicted of crimes they committed before turning 18, weighing rehabilitation and education. history
  • HB 23 — Lets an applicant have a licensed engineer review or inspect development documents, instead of waiting for the local government to do it. history
  • HB 3892 — Stops counties from limiting how many homes can be built per acre outside city limits, including through minimum lot sizes, lot widths or setbacks. history
  • HB 3520 — Rewrites the insurance a rideshare driver must carry, separating cover while they are logged on and waiting from cover while a passenger is in the car. history
