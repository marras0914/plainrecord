Suleman Lalani, Texas House District 76

Suleman Lalani represented Texas House District 76 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Suleman Lalani cast 56, and on the 43 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 5 times.

Where District 76 is

District 76 is in Fort Bend County, and 198,548 people lived in it at the 2020 Census.

Most of District 76 (52%) lives outside any city or named community, in unincorporated Fort Bend County.

Where they broke with their own party

  • SB 2185 — Requires school districts excused from running a bilingual programme to report which alternative language methods they use, so those methods count towards bilingual education funding. history
  • HB 2308 — Creates an 11-member advisory commission of state officials and legislators to study border security and illegal immigration. history
  • HB 23 — Lets an applicant have a licensed engineer review or inspect development documents, instead of waiting for the local government to do it. history
  • SB 3 — Bans hemp products containing THC — the ones sold in smoke shops and convenience stores — and adds licences, fees and criminal penalties for selling them. history
  • SB 14 — Creates a state office that reviews agency rules and recommends repealing ones it finds unnecessary. It also tells judges to decide for themselves what a law means, rather than accepting the agency’s reading. history
