Caroline Harris Davila, Texas House District 52

Caroline Harris Davila represented Texas House District 52 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Caroline Harris Davila cast 65, and on the 49 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 1 time.
