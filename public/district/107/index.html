Linda Garcia, Texas House District 107

Linda Garcia represented Texas House District 107 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Linda Garcia cast 63, and on the 49 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.

Where District 107 is

Where they broke with their own party

  • HB 121 — Lets the Texas Education Agency commission its own police officers, requires a district claiming a good cause exception from the armed security requirement to adopt an alternative standard such as a school marshal, and changes who sits on the state’s School Safety Center board. history
  • HB 3179 — Lets the county where the Llano and James rivers meet charge its own hotel tax, except at hotels a city already taxes. history
