Marc LaHood, Texas House District 121

Marc LaHood represented Texas House District 121 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Marc LaHood cast 61, and on the 47 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 5 times.

Where District 121 is

District 121 is in Bexar County, and 203,209 people lived in it at the 2020 Census.

  • San Antonio 70% of the district · 10% of San Antonio’s residents
  • Timberwood Park 7% of the district · 41% of Timberwood Park’s residents · unincorporated community
  • Alamo Heights 4% of the district · all of Alamo Heights
  • Terrell Hills 2% of the district · all of Terrell Hills
  • Olmos Park 1% of the district · all of Olmos Park

The other 15% of the district lives outside any city or named community.
