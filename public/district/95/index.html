Nicole Collier, Texas House District 95

Nicole Collier represented Texas House District 95 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Nicole Collier cast 65, and on the 50 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 3 times.
