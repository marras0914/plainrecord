Liz Campos, Texas House District 119

Liz Campos represented Texas House District 119 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Democrat. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Liz Campos cast 61, and on the 48 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.

Where District 119 is
