David Spiller, Texas House District 68

David Spiller represented Texas House District 68 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, David Spiller cast 66, and on the 51 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.
