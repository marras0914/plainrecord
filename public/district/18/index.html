Janis Holt, Texas House District 18

Janis Holt represented Texas House District 18 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. This page is the record: of the 67 votes this site publishes, Janis Holt cast 58, and on the 45 of those where the two parties took opposite sides, they voted against their own party 2 times.

Where District 18 is

District 18 spans 4 counties, and 202,990 people lived in it at the 2020 Census: Liberty County (45%), Hardin County (28%), Montgomery County (14%) and San Jacinto County (14%).

Most of District 18 (66%) lives outside any city or named community, in unincorporated parts of those counties.
