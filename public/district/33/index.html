Katrina Pierson, Texas House District 33

Katrina Pierson represented Texas House District 33 in the 89th Legislature (the 2025 session) and is a Republican. Where they broke with their own party

  • SB 2185 — Requires school districts excused from running a bilingual programme to report which alternative language methods they use, so those methods count towards bilingual education funding. history
  • SB 1563 — Requires the state to create training for county jailers on dealing with veterans in custody. history
  • HB 3892 — Stops counties from limiting how many homes can be built per acre outside city limits, including through minimum lot sizes, lot widths or setbacks. history
  • SB 1883 — Makes impact fees harder to impose or raise: approval now needs a two thirds vote of the governing body, a fee cannot be increased for three years, and the growth assumptions and capital plans must be published 60 days before the hearing rather than on the day of notice. history
  • HB 3179 — Lets the county where the Llano and James rivers meet charge its own hotel tax, except at hotels a city already taxes. history
