How every Texas House member voted on SB 14

Regulatory review. Creates a state office that reviews agency rules and recommends repealing ones it finds unnecessary. It also tells judges to decide for themselves what a law means, rather than accepting the agency’s reading.

The House voted 97 to 51. This is record vote 91 in the House Journal. Lieutenant Governor Dan Patrick named it a must-pass priority. Governor Greg Abbott signed it.
