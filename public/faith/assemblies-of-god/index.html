Immigration

Who may come to and stay in the United States, and how the law is enforced at the border and within it.

Official statements

The General Council of the Assemblies of God, August 14, 2006

Official statement on immigration

Says the borders should be secure so that immigration conforms to the law, supports comprehensive immigration reform reflecting human dignity, compassion and justice, and says ministry to everyone living or working in the country stands apart from questions of jurisdiction.

“we support comprehensive immigration reform that reflects human dignity, compassion, and justice”

This statement cites no Bible passage.

In Congress, 2025–26: bills it backed or opposed

The Assemblies of God (USA) has no Washington policy office, and ag.org posted no position on a bill in 2025-26.

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for comprehensive immigration reform with an attainable path to citizenship, keeping the Texas DREAM Act and passing a federal one, more family and employment visas, and fewer limits on asylum and Temporary Protected Status, and says being in the country without authorization is a civil offense, not a crime.

“Enact comprehensive immigration reform, including an attainable path to citizenship”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for securing the border by whatever means necessary, including barriers and a wall, a state Border Protection Unit with authority to repel crossings and deport, faster final rulings and deportation for people in the country illegally, and deportation of non-citizens arrested in violent protests.

“effectively secure the border through whatever means necessary”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for legislation that secures the border, reforms the asylum system, expands legal immigration, keeps families together with a pathway for long-term undocumented residents, and secures the future of the DACA program.

“secure the border, reform the asylum system, expand legal immigration”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for sealing the border, carrying out what it calls the largest deportation operation in American history, strengthening ICE, raising penalties for illegal entry and visa overstays, and reinstating the Remain in Mexico policy.

“Republicans will secure the Border, deport Illegal Aliens”

Full text

Abortion

Whether and when the law permits ending a pregnancy.

Official statements

Position paper adopted by the General Presbytery, August 2010

Sanctity of Human Life: Abortion and Reproductive Issues

Holds that human life from conception is to be protected, denies that a legal right to abortion confers a moral right, says historic Christian faith has usually favored the mother's life where childbirth would likely cause her death, and calls on Christians to seek legislative and judicial redress.

“from conception through death, is therefore to be valued, respected, nurtured, and protected”

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for reinstating the right to an abortion, repealing the Hyde Amendment, protecting IVF, requiring emergency rooms to provide stabilizing care including abortion when medically necessary, and barring penalties on people who travel for an abortion or help them.

“Expand access to contraception and reinstate the right to an abortion”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for legislation to abolish abortion by giving the unborn equal protection of the law from fertilization, and for barring public funds for elective abortion and the transport of women across state lines to obtain one, with a private right of action against those who help.

“We urge lawmakers to enact legislation to abolish abortion”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for national legislation to make Roe the law of the land again, repealing the Hyde Amendment, and protecting access to contraception and IVF, and describes the administration's position that federal emergency-care law preempts state abortion bans.

“we will pass national legislation to make Roe the law of the land again”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for opposing late-term abortion, says the 14th Amendment guarantees that no person can be denied life without due process and that the states are free to pass laws protecting that right, and supports mothers, prenatal care, access to birth control and IVF.

“We will oppose Late Term Abortion”

Full text

Poverty and welfare

Public assistance for people with low incomes.

Official statements

No official statement

There is no position paper or official statement on poverty or public assistance.

In Congress, 2025–26: bills it backed or opposed

The Assemblies of God (USA) has no Washington policy office, and ag.org posted no position on a bill in 2025-26.

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for a living wage indexed to inflation for all Texas workers, including tipped workers, and for removing outside work requirements for SNAP benefits.

“All Texas workers should receive a living wage indexed to inflation”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for abolishing all federal welfare programs, as not an appropriate role for the federal government, and for repealing minimum wage and prevailing wage laws.

“We support the abolition of all federal welfare programs”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for raising the federal minimum wage to at least $15 an hour and restoring the expanded Child Tax Credit and Earned Income Tax Credit.

“We'll work to finally raise the federal minimum wage to at least $15-an-hour”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

The 2024 RNC platform has no plank on poverty or public assistance. Its nearest commitments are no cuts to Social Security and Medicare, and making permanent the 2017 tax law, including its larger Child Tax Credit.

Guns

Who may own firearms and how the law regulates them.

Official statements

No official statement

There is no position paper or official statement on guns.

In Congress, 2025–26: bills it backed or opposed

The Assemblies of God (USA) has no Washington policy office, and ag.org posted no position on a bill in 2025-26.

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for preserving Second Amendment rights while adding safeguards against suicide, mass murder, accidents and interpersonal violence, including background checks for gun-show and private sales, and lifting limits on federal research into gun violence.

“supports the right of law-abiding Americans to own and bear arms”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for repealing or nullifying the National Firearms Act and the Gun Control Act, unrestricted constitutional carry, aligning the Texas Constitution with the Second Amendment's language, and making businesses that bar licensed carriers liable for their safety.

“Repeal and/or nullify the National Firearms Act of 1934”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for universal background checks, a renewed ban on assault weapons and high-capacity magazines, and required safe storage, and cites the administration's red flag laws, narrowing of the "boyfriend loophole" and action on ghost guns.

“We will once again ban assault weapons and high-capacity magazines”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for defending the right to keep and bear arms, as one of the constitutional freedoms in the platform's list of 20 commitments; the platform has no other plank on guns.

“THE RIGHT TO KEEP AND BEAR ARMS”

Full text

Creation care and the environment

Pollution, conservation and climate.

Official statements

No official statement

There is no position paper on the environment. The 2015 position paper Church Mission and Peacemaking refers to a "perspective paper" on environmental protection, but no such paper is published on ag.org.

In Congress, 2025–26: bills it backed or opposed

The Assemblies of God (USA) has no Washington policy office, and ag.org posted no position on a bill in 2025-26.

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for reducing greenhouse gas emissions through clean energy, alternative fuel vehicles and efficient buildings, rapid decarbonization, and a stronger EPA that sets and meets a net zero carbon emissions goal, and states that climate change is real.

“Reduce greenhouse gas emissions by supporting policies that develop clean energy resources”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for opposing climate change initiatives that obstruct business interests and private property use, including Executive Order 14008, the 30x30 conservation initiative, Net Zero mandates and declarations of a climate emergency.

“We oppose environmentalism, or “climate change” initiatives”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for continuing the clean energy investments of the Inflation Reduction Act, faster permitting for clean energy, more clean energy on public lands, electrified ports and zero-emissions freight, and lower emissions from buildings and heavy industry.

“We'll keep pushing to reduce emissions from America's buildings and heavy industry”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for lifting restrictions on oil and gas production, ending the Green New Deal, canceling the electric vehicle mandate and cutting regulations, and pledges to preserve the nation's natural treasures and restore what it calls genuine conservation efforts.

“We will DRILL, BABY, DRILL and we will become Energy Independent”

Full text

Healthcare

Who pays for medical care and how people get access to it.

Official statements

No official statement

There is no position paper or official statement on healthcare policy.

In Congress, 2025–26: bills it backed or opposed

The Assemblies of God (USA) has no Washington policy office, and ag.org posted no position on a bill in 2025-26.

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for expanding Medicaid, protecting Medicare benefits, making prescription drugs affordable for all, and helping small businesses afford health insurance for their employees.

“We must expand Medicaid, protect Medicare benefits, and make prescription drugs affordable for all”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for building state reserves sufficient for Texas to exit the federal Medicaid program, directing budget surpluses to a Texas Health Savings Account until then, and opposing mandates for treatment, vaccination or masks and health insurance surcharges.

“enabling the state to develop reserves sufficient to exit the federal Medicaid program”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for protecting and expanding the Affordable Care Act, extending Medicaid-like coverage in states that have not expanded Medicaid, lowering drug prices through more transparency from pharmacy benefit managers, and ending the waiting list for Medicaid home- and community-based services.

“protect and expand the Affordable Care Act”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for more transparency, choice and competition in health care, expanded access to affordable health care and prescription drug options, and protecting Medicare with no cuts.

“expand access to new Affordable Healthcare and prescription drug options”

Full text

Criminal justice

Policing, sentencing and prisons.

Official statements

No official statement

There is no position paper or official statement on criminal justice.

In Congress, 2025–26: bills it backed or opposed

The Assemblies of God (USA) has no Washington policy office, and ag.org posted no position on a bill in 2025-26.

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for ending the use of criminal statutes and incarceration to punish problems such as addiction and homelessness, addressing bias in the justice system, and training police to build trust in diverse communities.

“End the punishment of social problems such as addiction and homelessness”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for basing bail only on a person's danger to society, risk of flight and criminal history, enforcing laws uniformly with punishment that fits the crime, and never using law enforcement to target people for political purposes.

“ensure bail in Texas is based only on a person’s danger to society”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for passing the George Floyd Justice in Policing Act, ending state-level use of private prisons, restricting solitary confinement and chokeholds, automatically sealing juvenile records, and supporting rehabilitation and reentry.

“Democrats will end state-level use of private prisons and detention centers”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for replenishing police departments, protecting officers from lawsuits it calls frivolous, locking up violent offenders, and cutting federal funding to sanctuary jurisdictions.

“restore safety in our neighborhoods by replenishing Police Departments”

Full text
