What scripture each tradition cites

Traditions often cite scripture for their positions, and sometimes the same passage for opposite answers. This page lists what each tradition's statement on an issue cites, grouped by the side its words take on the platforms' question. The site does not say which reading is right.

How these pages are checked. Every summary is this site’s own; no one from the traditions reviews them. A program checks every quote against a saved copy of its source, every summary links to the full text, and every placement follows the rules on the methodology page. A better source corrects anything here.

Each list is every passage a statement cites, not only those it gives for this point. Where a document covers several issues, only its section on this issue counts. None of the four party platforms cites scripture.
