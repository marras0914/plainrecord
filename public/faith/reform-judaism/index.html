Immigration

Who may come to and stay in the United States, and how the law is enforced at the border and within it.

Official statements

Adopted by the Central Conference of American Rabbis, April 22, 2026

Central Conference of American Rabbis Resolution on Immigration & Due Process

Opposes immigration enforcement at or near houses of worship, hospitals and schools except in urgent circumstances, denounces deportations without due process, condemns raids by masked immigration officers, and calls for legislation that balances security with a humane welcome.

“Denounces deportations without due process procedures”

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for comprehensive immigration reform with an attainable path to citizenship, keeping the Texas DREAM Act and passing a federal one, more family and employment visas, and fewer limits on asylum and Temporary Protected Status, and says being in the country without authorization is a civil offense, not a crime.

“Enact comprehensive immigration reform, including an attainable path to citizenship”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for securing the border by whatever means necessary, including barriers and a wall, a state Border Protection Unit with authority to repel crossings and deport, faster final rulings and deportation for people in the country illegally, and deportation of non-citizens arrested in violent protests.

“effectively secure the border through whatever means necessary”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for legislation that secures the border, reforms the asylum system, expands legal immigration, keeps families together with a pathway for long-term undocumented residents, and secures the future of the DACA program.

“secure the border, reform the asylum system, expand legal immigration”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for sealing the border, carrying out what it calls the largest deportation operation in American history, strengthening ICE, raising penalties for illegal entry and visa overstays, and reinstating the Remain in Mexico policy.

“Republicans will secure the Border, deport Illegal Aliens”

Abortion

Whether and when the law permits ending a pregnancy.

Official statements

Adopted by the Central Conference of American Rabbis with the Women's Rabbinic Network, April 18, 2024

Resolution Affirming Our Commitment to Broad, Accessible Reproductive & Sexual Health Care

Calls on its members and communities to affirm the legal right of a pregnant person to choose to carry or end a pregnancy free from government interference, along with the right to birth control and to comprehensive sex education.

“legal right of a pregnant person to make their own informed, medically guided choice”

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for reinstating the right to an abortion, repealing the Hyde Amendment, protecting IVF, requiring emergency rooms to provide stabilizing care including abortion when medically necessary, and barring penalties on people who travel for an abortion or help them.

“Expand access to contraception and reinstate the right to an abortion”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for legislation to abolish abortion by giving the unborn equal protection of the law from fertilization, and for barring public funds for elective abortion and the transport of women across state lines to obtain one, with a private right of action against those who help.

“We urge lawmakers to enact legislation to abolish abortion”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for national legislation to make Roe the law of the land again, repealing the Hyde Amendment, and protecting access to contraception and IVF, and describes the administration's position that federal emergency-care law preempts state abortion bans.

“we will pass national legislation to make Roe the law of the land again”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for opposing late-term abortion, says the 14th Amendment guarantees that no person can be denied life without due process and that the states are free to pass laws protecting that right, and supports mothers, prenatal care, access to birth control and IVF.

“We will oppose Late Term Abortion”

The death penalty

Whether the state may execute people convicted of crimes.

Official statements

Adopted by the Central Conference of American Rabbis at its 90th Annual Convention, 1979

Capital Punishment

Restates the conference's opposition to the death penalty, first stated in 1958 and 1960, says no evidence shows that it deters crime, and opposes it under all circumstances.

“We oppose capital punishment under all circumstances.”

This statement cites no scripture or rabbinic text.

Guns

Official statements

Adopted by the Central Conference of American Rabbis, August 11, 2015

CCAR Resolution on Gun Violence

Urges members of its congregations and communities to demand that Congress enact gun violence prevention legislation, and calls on governments that buy guns to use that buying power to press manufacturers for safer dealer networks and safer gun technology.

The party platforms

Texas

Texas Democratic Party, 2024 platform

Calls for preserving Second Amendment rights while adding safeguards against suicide, mass murder, accidents and interpersonal violence, including background checks for gun-show and private sales, and lifting limits on federal research into gun violence.

“supports the right of law-abiding Americans to own and bear arms”

Full text

Republican Party of Texas, 2024 platform

Calls for repealing or nullifying the National Firearms Act and the Gun Control Act, unrestricted constitutional carry, aligning the Texas Constitution with the Second Amendment's language, and making businesses that bar licensed carriers liable for their safety.

“Repeal and/or nullify the National Firearms Act of 1934”

Full text

National

Democratic National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for universal background checks, a renewed ban on assault weapons and high-capacity magazines, and required safe storage, and cites the administration's red flag laws, narrowing of the "boyfriend loophole" and action on ghost guns.

“We will once again ban assault weapons and high-capacity magazines”

Full text

Republican National Committee, 2024 platform

Calls for defending the right to keep and bear arms, as one of the constitutional freedoms in the platform's list of 20 commitments; the platform has no other plank on guns.

“THE RIGHT TO KEEP AND BEAR ARMS”

Healthcare

Official statements

Adopted by the Central Conference of American Rabbis, March 23, 2017
