What they backed in Congress

Words are one thing; what a tradition's advocacy office asks of Congress is another. For each issue, this page lists the bills each tradition's own office publicly backed or opposed since January 2025, beside the tradition's official position. It counts nothing and grades no one.

Not yet reviewed. No one from the traditions on these pages has reviewed what they say. Every summary is this site's own, and each one links to the full text so you can check it.

Entries reviewed so far: 0 of 83.

Only the body's own letters to Congress, action alerts, statements, legislative agenda or endorsement list count, and only where it names a bill and takes a side. Federal lobbying reports cannot answer this: the law does not count a church's contacts with Congress as lobbying, so most of these bodies never file them. Every quote and every bill name was checked against a saved copy of the document.

Floor votes: under each bill that reached a recorded vote in this Congress, each chamber’s final vote, with how each party voted, from the House Clerk’s and the Senate’s roll calls. A vote to begin debate is shown only where it stopped a bill before any vote on passage. Of the 1,608 recorded floor votes so far, only those on the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, Laken Riley Act and Born-Alive Abortion Survivors Protection Act were on a bill named here. Nothing marks a party as voting with or against a tradition.
